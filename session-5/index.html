The School of AI · ERA v5 · Session 5

The mixture is the model.

A corpus doesn't decide what a model becomes — the data mixture does: how much of each capability it sees, and in what order. This is the V5 plan over a fixed 3.0T-token budget, composed backward from the benchmarks it must win. And because a data decision is only a hypothesis until a cheap experiment tests it, the plan ships with a proxy that was actually run.

Full plan on GitHub · the submission The proxy · mixture_plan.py

Model: V5 ~40B India-first (dense or small-active MoE) · ~75 tok/param · anneal = last 2.5% (~75B tok)

01The main pretraining mixture

Seven lanes over 3.0T tokens. Each is tied to the benchmark it should win and sized against real supply — and where unique tokens don't cover the target, the plan says so instead of hiding it.

The thing to notice first. The two capabilities this project exists for — agentic and deep reasoning — are exactly the two with almost no natural supply. 87% of the reasoning lane and 75% of the agentic lane must be built (distilled traces; harvested Cursor/Claude-Code trajectories). That's a budgeted synthesis programme, not wishful accounting.

02The Indic lane, across four provenance tiers

Verified native Indic is thin — a 390B target cannot be met from verified sources alone, exactly as the session warns. That doesn't invalidate the target; it defines how much must be translated and synthesised.

03The always-on floor — and proof it's needed

An OPUS-style selector (the session cites ~8× efficiency) improves compute use, but its proxy is English/code-benchmark-driven, so it undervalues Indic (off-benchmark) and agentic (looks like logs). The floor reserves 8% Indic · 3% agentic · 3% reasoning of every batch, outside the selector.

Experiment B simulates one selection round (100k candidate batches, keep the top 50% by proxy score). The result is the session's central lesson, measured:

Floor off, the two lanes the project is for collapse to zero. Floor on, they hold at 8% and 3%. Asserted in the lecture; measured here.

04The anneal — a separate preset, and a reserve

The final 2.5% (~75B tokens) runs a different mixture on the hardest, cleanest data with the learning rate decayed. General web falls sharply; scarce lanes are upsampled.

LaneMainAnneal
General web40%12%
Code18%20%
Math / STEM14%12%
Reasoning (long)6%14%
Agentic (Tier-A)4%18%
Indic13%22%
Long context5%2%

Anneal reserve — declared now, spent only at the end. ~40B tokens held out of the main run entirely: the best 30% of agentic trajectories, the longest verified reasoning traces, the highest-quality verified Indic, and the hardest verified code patches.

This reserve is the reason the anneal can exist. If the selector spends the best data early, there is nothing special left to cool down on.

05Curriculum — order, length, difficulty

Five stages; general text first, long sequences last, difficulty rising within each. Every transition is a warm-up band (~5–10B tokens of diffusion) so the gradient norm stays ~0.2 and never spikes.

Difficulty bands — one concrete example each

  • L0 "A for apple"; single-fact sentences; 1-step arithmetic
  • L1 a GSM8K word problem; a single function call
  • L2 MATH level 1–3; a self-contained one-file program
  • L3 MATH level 4–5; LeetCode-medium; a multi-file edit
  • L4 FrontierMath; a SWE-bench-Verified multi-step agentic fix

Reasoning-length bands — the effort dial

  • low ≤256 tok · "capital of France?" → direct answer
  • medium 256–2k · a GSM8K problem with a short chain
  • high 2k–8k · an AIME problem exploring 2–3 approaches
  • ultra 8k–32k+ · olympiad problem with verification & backtracking

06The experiment, and what a reviewer should push on

Two load-bearing claims are already tested cheaply and deterministically in mixture_plan.py. A real 1B/3B proxy replaces the modelled assumptions with measured numbers.

A — supply is real or honestly synthetic. ✓ Math at its ×3 rep ceiling; reasoning & agentic flagged majority-synthetic; Indic closes only across 4 tiers. No lane quietly over-allocated.

B — the floor stops starvation. ✓ Indic/agentic 0.0% → 8%/3% when the floor is switched on.

What a hostile reviewer should attack (stated, not hidden):

· inventory numbers are order-of-magnitude — pin exact dedup'd counts before the run.

· the synthesis programme (87% / 75%) is the real bet — it needs a quality gate and a ~70:30 real:synthetic ceiling.

· OPUS proxy-alignment is modelled, not measured — the 1B proxy fixes that.

Metric — capability parity, not average. Score = min over lanes of (benchmark / target), so you can't win by starving a lane. Confirm if no lane regresses below its floor and Indic rises without >2 pt MMLU loss; refute if Indic gains cost >2 pt general knowledge, or agentic fails to move (a supply problem, not a share problem).
